# Affiliate Site Generator: Build Income-Producing Content Sites

This skill provides a complete framework for building affiliate content sites from scratch, including niche research, site architecture, content generation, product reviews, and SEO optimization strategies.

## Part 1: Niche Research & Validation

### Step 1: Identify Market Opportunities

```markdown
## Affiliate Niche Evaluation Framework

Search volume targets:
- HOW-TO keywords: 1K-10K monthly searches
- PRODUCT REVIEW keywords: 500-5K monthly searches
- COMPARISON keywords: 100-1K monthly searches

Amazon affiliate requirements:
- 3 sales in 180 days (to stay active)
- Commission: 1-10% depending on category
- Best categories: Electronics (3-10%), Sports (10%), Tools (8%)

Competition analysis:
- Domain rating (DR) of top 3 results <50 = opportunity
- DR 30-40 = moderate competition
- DR < 30 = low competition (build here)
```

### Step 2: Validate Monetization

```markdown
## Revenue Model Validation

Example: "Dog Beds" niche

Volume: 8,000 monthly searches
Top product: $50-150 (good margin)
Commission: 5-8% through affiliate programs
Estimated revenue per sale: $2.50-12

Traffic needed for $1K/month:
- Assume 1% conversion (conservative)
- Assume $5 average commission
- Need: $1,000 / $5 = 200 conversions
- At 1% conversion: 20,000 visits/month
- Realistic with 50-100 content pieces

Validation: ✓ Viable niche
```

### Content Pillar Strategy

```markdown
## Content Pillars (12 main topics)

1. Product Reviews (40% of content)
   - Individual brand reviews
   - Category reviews (memory foam, elevated, raised)

2. Buying Guides (25% of content)
   - Size guide

3. Comparisons (20% of content)
```

## Part 4: SEO Optimization Strategy

### Keyword Research Framework

```markdown
## Keyword Targeting by Search Intent

### Informational (Top of Funnel)
Keywords: "How to choose dog bed", "Best dog bed material"
Target: Blog posts, guides
Volume: 5K-50K
Competition: High
Goal: Drive traffic, build authority

### Commercial (Middle of Funnel)
Keywords: "Best dog beds for [type]"
Target: Category pages, reviews
Volume: 1K-10K
Competition: Medium
Goal: Warm prospects to product consideration

### Transactional (Bottom of Funnel)
Keywords: "[Brand] [Model] dog bed", "[Brand] dog bed price"
Target: Individual reviews, comparison pages
Volume: 100-1K
Competition: Low
Goal: Convert searchers to buyers

### Long-tail Opportunities
Keywords: "Best memory foam dog bed for large dogs with arthritis"
Volume: 50-200
Competition: Very low
Goal: Capture specific intent, high conversion potential
```

### On-Page SEO Checklist

```markdown
## Article Optimization (Every Article)

[ ] Keyword appears in H1 tag
[ ] Primary keyword in first 100 words
[ ] 2-3 variations of primary keyword throughout
[ ] Meta description includes primary keyword
[ ] URL slug uses primary keyword
[ ] Images have alt text with keywords
[ ] Internal links (3-5) to related content
[ ] Outbound links (2-3) to authority sources
[ ] Word count 2,000+ for reviews, 1,500+ for guides
[ ] Reading time under 10 minutes (scanned easily)
```

### Technical SEO Requirements

```markdown
## Site-Level SEO

[ ] Mobile responsive (100% Lighthouse)
[ ] Fast load time (<2 seconds, Core Web Vitals pass)
[ ] SSL certificate (HTTPS)
[ ] Sitemap.xml generated
[ ] Robots.txt configured
[ ] Internal link structure (pillar → clusters)
[ ] Breadcrumb navigation
[ ] Schema markup (Article, Review, Product)
[ ] No duplicate content
[ ] Structured data for reviews/ratings
```

## Part 7: Content Refresh & Scaling

### Refresh Schedule

Every quarter, update:
- [ ] Best-of list articles (new products available?)
- [ ] Price and commission information
- [ ] Competitor comparison articles

### Scaling Opportunities

Once established (12+ months):

1. **Expand to related niches**
   - Dog toys, dog food, dog grooming
   - Build network of 5-10 related sites

2. **Build email list**
   - "Dog bed buying guide" lead magnet
   - 10,000+ subscribers = $1,000-5,000/month (sponsorships, promotions)

3. **Create video content**
   - Product reviews on YouTube
   - Much lower saturation than blogs
   - Long-tail keyword opportunities

4. **Sell digital products**
   - "Dog bed selection quiz"
   - "Complete dog bed buyer's guide" ($17-27)
   - Builds email list while monetizing
