# Account-Based Marketing (ABM)

ABM flips traditional marketing on its head. Instead of casting a wide net, you treat high-value accounts as markets of one. This guide covers the complete ABM system.

## ABM vs Traditional Marketing

**Traditional Marketing (Spray & Pray)**
- Cast wide net (all of market)
- Attract many leads
- Sales qualifies leads
- Conversion: 1-5%
- CAC: Medium
- Typical: Thousands of leads, many low-fit

**ABM (Targeted & Personalized)**
- Target small number of high-fit accounts
- Attract qualified decision-makers
- Marketing aligns with sales on accounts
- Conversion: 15-40% (much higher)
- CAC: Higher per lead, but better ROI (higher LTV)
- Typical: Dozens of accounts, high conversion

**When to Use ABM**
- Enterprise deals ($100K+ ARR potential)
- Long sales cycles (3-6 months)
- Multiple decision-makers
- High-touch sales model
- B2B SaaS with 50-500 target companies

**When NOT to Use ABM**
- SMB market (low-touch, many targets)
- High-volume, low-price point
- Short sales cycles
- Self-serve model

## Building Your Account List

**Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)**

Define characteristics of your best customers:
- Company size: 100-500 employees
- Industry: SaaS, MarTech, FinTech
- Revenue: $10M-$500M
- Geography: US, Western Europe
- Typical use case: 2-3 specific use cases

Example:
"Mid-market B2B SaaS companies ($20-200M revenue, 50-500 employees) with 15+ person marketing teams spending $2M+/year on marketing tools and struggling with integration complexity."

**Account Selection** (Tiered Approach)

Tier 1 (10-20 accounts): Highest value
- Target: $500K+ ARR potential
- Tactics: 1-to-1 personalized outreach, executive sponsorship
- Resources: AE + ABM specialist per account
- Timeline: 6-12 months

Tier 2 (50-100 accounts): High value
- Target: $100K-500K ARR potential
- Tactics: Personalized email, display ads, content
- Resources: AE + 1 ABM specialist for 10-15 accounts
- Timeline: 3-6 months

Tier 3 (500+ accounts): Fit, but not personalized
- Target: $50K-100K ARR potential
- Tactics: Automated campaigns, LinkedIn ads
- Resources: Automated nurturing, minimal manual
- Timeline: 1-3 months

**Finding High-Value Accounts**

Data sources:
- Your CRM (customers similar to best customers)
- LinkedIn (company targeting, employee searching)
- Clearbit/ZoomInfo (company data enrichment)
- G2/Capterra (competitors' customers)
- SEC filings (funding announcements, IPOs)
- News (hiring sprees, funding, acquisitions)

Qualification:
- Does ICP match? (Check company size, industry, revenue)
- Decision-maker accessible? (Can you reach them?)
- Pain point fit? (Does your solution solve their problem?)
- Buying timeline? (When will they buy?)
- Competition? (Are competitors already in?)

## Personalized Content for ABM

**Content by Buyer Journey Stage**

Awareness (Account is aware of problem, not aware of solution)
- Education: "5 ways enterprises structure their MarTech stack"
- Data: Report on industry trends
- Format: Blog, whitepaper, webinar
- Personalization: Mention their industry, company

Consideration (Account evaluating solutions)
- Comparison: "Comparing integration approaches: Custom vs API-first"
- ROI calculator: "Calculate your integration savings"
- Case studies: From companies similar to them
- Format: Interactive tools, in-depth guides
- Personalization: Speak to their use case, company size

Decision (Account evaluating your solution)
- Product demo: Customized to their needs
- Reference call: Intro to customer in same industry
- Pricing/proposal: Tailored to their usage
- Implementation timeline: Specific to them
- Format: Video, call, personalized proposal

**Content Personalization Tactics**

By company:
- Use company name/logo (not generic)
- Reference their specific challenges
- Show relevant case studies (similar companies)
- Mention their industry or role

By role:
- CMO content: Focus on strategy, ROI, team productivity
- MarOps content: Focus on efficiency, integration, data
- VP Sales content: Focus on alignment, revenue impact
- CRO content: Focus on growth, metrics, dashboards

**Content Format Ideas for ABM**

1. **Personalized emails** (with data about them)
   - "I noticed [Company] is using [competitor], here's where you might be missing out..."
   - "We helped [Competitor] reduce their integration time by 70%..."

2. **Account-specific ads** (LinkedIn, display)
   - Only show to employees at target accounts
   - Use their language, their problems
   - Dynamic: Show different creatives to different roles

3. **Personalized landing pages**
   - Variable content based on company (Clearbit)
   - Case studies from their industry
   - ROI calculator for their company size

4. **Thought leadership for key decision-makers**
   - Email their CEO an article on their industry
   - Tag them in LinkedIn posts relevant to their business
   - Invite to exclusive webinar

## Intent Data Strategy

**Intent Signals** (Shows they're actively buying)

Buying signals:
- Website visit (especially product/pricing pages)
- Content download (especially comparison guides)
- Time on site (high engagement = interest)
- Multiple pages visited (not one-off)
- Job postings (hiring for function they'd use your tool for)
- Funding announcement (expansion, new budget)
- Press coverage (product launch, growth)

**Intent Data Tools**
- 6sense (Intent data, firmographics, contact info)
- Demandbase (Intent signals, target lists)
- ZoomInfo (Company data, intent signals)
- LinkedIn (Job changes, company updates)
- Drift (Website behavior tracking)
- HubSpot (Website visitor tracking via ads)

**Intent-Based Campaign Example**

Trigger: Detected [Company] is in market for integration platform
Action:
1. AE reaches out directly (personalized email)
2. Email sequence starts (5 emails, daily)
3. LinkedIn ads appear (only to [Company] domain)
4. Retargeting ads on their site visits (product demo CTA)
5. After 10 days: Executive outreach from sponsor

Result: 40% response rate to cold outreach (vs 2% without intent)

## Sales & Marketing Alignment

**Shared Account Plan**

For each Tier 1 account, create joint plan:

Account: [Company Name]
Target: Close by [Date]

Decision Makers:
- [Name]: CMO (Budget holder, priority: ROI)
- [Name]: VP Marketing (User, priority: integration)
- [Name]: Director of MarTech (Implementation, priority: ease)

Current state:
- Using [Competitor]
- Pain point: X costs $200K/year, wastes 10 hours/week
- Budget: $500K allocated for MarTech

Marketing plan:
- Send [Name] article on integration ROI
- Invite all 3 to webinar on [topic]
- Account-specific landing page
- LinkedIn ads to their company

Sales plan:
- [AE] schedules coffee with CMO
- [AE] asks for product demo meeting
- [AE] prepares ROI calculator
- [AE] coordinates reference call

Timeline:
- Week 1: Awareness (content, outreach)
- Week 3: Engagement (demo request)
- Week 5: Evaluation (competitive demo)
- Week 8: Decision (proposal, negotiation)

**ABM Metrics to Track**

- Account engagement (across marketing and sales touchpoints)
- Pipeline influence (accounts that became opportunities)
- Win rate (closed deals from ABM accounts)
- Deal size (average ARR from ABM accounts)
- Sales cycle length (days from first touch to close)
- CAC (cost per ABM account)
- ROI (revenue from ABM / ABM investment)

## ABM Campaign Structure

**Email Sequence for Tier 1 Account** (Personalized)

Email 1 (Day 1): Why we reached out
- Specific reason (not generic)
- Insight about their company
- No pitch
- Goal: Get them to open next email

Email 2 (Day 3): Problem validation
- Share stat or insight relevant to them
- Frame as question: "Are you experiencing this?"
- Still no pitch
- Goal: Get response

Email 3 (Day 5): Social proof
- Case study from similar company
- Results they care about (not features)
- Softest ask: "Worth a conversation?"
- Goal: Demo request

Email 4 (Day 10): Different angle
- If no response, try different angle
- Different angle: Talk to different buyer
- Or: Different problem angle (if first didn't resonate)
- Goal: Engagement

Email 5 (Day 15): Final attempt
- "I'll assume this isn't a priority, but if it is..."
- Leave door open for future
- Archive if no response, revisit quarterly

**LinkedIn Strategy for ABM**

- Connect with decision-makers at target account
- Personalized message with value (not sales pitch)
- Share relevant content (tag them, ask opinion)
- Engage with their posts (thoughtful comments)
- Invite to exclusive event or webinar
- Goal: Establish credibility, build relationship

## 6-Month ABM Roadmap

**Month 1: Planning & Setup**
- Define ICP (ideal customer profile)
- Build initial target account list (50 accounts)
- Set up CRM for account tracking
- Create account plan template

**Month 2: Foundation**
- Tier accounts (10 Tier 1, 20 Tier 2, 20 Tier 3)
- Enrich account data (add contacts, job titles)
- Create 3-5 personalized landing pages
- Develop Tier 1 account plans

**Month 3: Campaign Launch**
- Begin Tier 1 email sequences
- Launch account-specific ads
- Sales starts outreach to Tier 1
- Weekly alignment meetings (sales + marketing)

**Month 4: Engagement**
- Track engagement metrics
- Iterate based on response
- Expand to Tier 2 accounts
- First demos/meetings happening

**Month 5: Optimization**
- Double down on what's working
- Adjust messaging based on feedback
- Score accounts by engagement
- Prepare proposals for active opportunities

**Month 6: Momentum**
- First Tier 1 deals close
- Calculate ROI
- Case study development
- Plan next wave of Tier 1 accounts

ABM requires upfront work but delivers exceptional ROI for enterprise deals.
