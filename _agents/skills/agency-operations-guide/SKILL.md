# Agency Operations Guide

You are an expert in service agency business models. Your goal is to help users build sustainable, profitable agencies.

## Agency Business Model Overview

**Core formula:**
- Sell professional services
- Deliver to client
- Repeat with more clients
- Eventually: Systematize and scale

**Challenges:**
- Time-bound revenue (can't exceed staff hours)
- Client acquisition cost
- Retaining quality talent
- Positioning against competitors

---

## Positioning Your Agency

### Specialization > Generalization

**Bad:** "We do marketing"
**Good:** "We help B2B SaaS companies reduce CAC through SEO and content"

**Why specialization wins:**
- Easier to market to
- Premium pricing (20-40% more)
- Better client fit
- Stronger reputation
- Easier to systematize

### Positioning Formula

```
We help [specific type of business]
with [specific problem]
using [your unique approach/method]
so they can [achieve outcome]
```

**Example:**
"We help bootstrapped SaaS founders reduce customer acquisition cost from $500 to $150 through lean GTM strategies, so they can grow without raising capital."

## Service Offerings

### Service Tiers

**Tier 1: Project-Based ($5K-$25K)**
- One-off projects
- Easier to sell
- Lower commitment
- Examples: Website redesign, campaign launch

**Tier 2: Monthly Retainer ($3K-$15K/mo)**
- Ongoing work
- Predictable revenue
- Better client fit
- Examples: Content creation, ongoing optimization

**Tier 3: Fractional Executive ($8K-$30K+/mo)**
- High-level strategic work
- Deepest relationships
- Highest margins
- Examples: CMO, COO, growth advisor

### Bundling Strategy

```
Small package: Website audit ($2K)
↓
Medium package: Design + implementation ($10K)
↓
Large package: Ongoing optimization + strategy ($5K/mo)
```

**Result:** Easier to upsell from small to large

## Pricing Models

### Value-Based Pricing

**Price by value delivered, not hours**

```
Client saves $100K/year from your work
Charge 20-30% of savings = $20K-$30K annually
```

**Advantages:**
- Aligns incentives (you want them to win)
- Much higher margins
- No race to bottom on pricing

**Challenges:**
- Harder to sell initially
- Need strong proof of ROI
- Requires consulting approach

### Hourly Rates

**Not recommended, but common**

```
Freelancer: $50-$150/hour
Agency: $100-$300/hour
Premium agency: $300-$1000/hour
```

**Problem:** Incentivizes slow work, not results

### Monthly Retainers

**Predictable revenue, sustainable for client**

```
Typical breakdown:
- 20% onboarding (setup, systems)
- 60% execution (deliverables)
- 20% optimization (testing, improvement)
```

**Pricing:** $3K-$15K depending on scope and your tier

## Getting Clients

### Inbound Channels

**Referrals (best):**
- Build reputation
- Word-of-mouth
- 30-40% of agencies' new clients
- Focus on delivering amazing work

**Content:**
- Blog posts on expertise
- Speaking
- Thought leadership
- Build audience of potential clients

**Partnerships:**
- Complementary agencies
- Referral relationships
- Co-marketing

### Outbound Channels

**Direct outreach:**
- Warm intros (through connections)
- Email sequences
- LinkedIn
- Low response but low cost

**Paid ads:**
- Google ads for high-intent search
- LinkedIn ads for targeting executives
- Facebook/Instagram for brand awareness

## Delivery & Systems

### Service Delivery Model

**Project phases:**
1. **Discovery (10% of time)** - Understand client, set goals
2. **Strategy (15%)** - Plan approach, get approval
3. **Execution (60%)** - Do the work
4. **Optimization (10%)** - Review, improve, report
5. **Transition (5%)** - Hand off, document

### Systematizing Your Work

**Create processes for:**
- Client onboarding
- Kickoff meetings
- Regular reporting
- Communication cadence
- Deliverable handoff
- Offboarding

**Benefits:**
- Quality consistency
- Faster execution
- Team can replicate
- Can hire contractors

### Communication Rhythm

**Weekly:** 30-min sync with client (status update)
**Bi-weekly:** Deeper strategy session (planning)
**Monthly:** Executive review (results, ROI)
**Quarterly:** Strategic planning (goals, roadmap)

## Retaining Clients

### Client Success Framework

**Make clients successful:**
1. Deliver more than promised (overdeliver)
2. Regular communication (no surprises)
3. Proactive optimization (don't wait for problems)
4. Quarterly business reviews (show impact)
5. Advocate for their business (not just your work)

### Retention Metrics

- **Churn rate:** % of clients lost per month (target: <5%)
- **Expansion rate:** % of clients upgrading (target: >20%)
- **NPS:** Client satisfaction (target: >50)

### Common Reasons Clients Leave

1. **Perceived lack of results** - Fix: Better reporting
2. **Poor communication** - Fix: More frequent touchpoints
3. **Personality clash** - Fix: Better client fit upfront
4. **Budget constraints** - Fix: Demonstrate ROI clearly
5. **Completed their goal** - Fix: Identify new needs proactively

## Team & Scaling

### Building Your Team

**Stages:**
1. **Solo (0-1 contractor)** - You do everything
2. **Small team (1-3 people)** - Hire for weak areas first
3. **Growing (4-10 people)** - Hire managers, build processes
4. **Scaled (10+ people)** - Multiple teams, standardized delivery

### Key Roles

**First hires (by this order):**
1. **Account manager** - Client relationships, admin
2. **Executor** - Doing the work (your expertise)
3. **Operations** - Systems, processes, financials
4. **Manager** - Oversee team (when 5+ people)

### Contractor vs. Employee

**Contractors (start here):**
- Variable cost
- No benefits/overhead
- Flexible
- No equity alignment
- Less control

**Employees (scale):**
- Fixed cost
- Expensive (2x contract cost)
- Committed
- Can give equity/career path
- More control and culture

## Metrics That Matter

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Monthly Recurring Revenue | Predictable monthly income | Growing 10%+ |
| Client Acquisition Cost | Cost to land client | <3 months of revenue |
| Lifetime Value | Total revenue per client | >$50K |
| Utilization | % of time on billable work | 70-80% |
| Gross Margin | Revenue minus direct costs | 60-70% |
| Churn | % of clients lost monthly | <5% |

## Common Agency Mistakes

| Mistake | Impact | Fix |
|---------|--------|-----|
| Unclear positioning | Hard to market | Specialize deeply |
| Underpricing | Unsustainable margins | Value-based pricing |
| Poor client fit | High churn, unhappy clients | Be selective upfront |
| Overcommitting | Quality suffers, team burned | Say no to bad fits |
| No processes | Can't scale, quality inconsistent | Document everything |
| No communication | Clients unhappy, churn | Weekly touchpoints |

## Agency Growth Roadmap

### Year 1: Foundation
- [ ] Define niche/positioning
- [ ] Land first 3-5 clients
- [ ] Create delivery process
- [ ] Establish pricing

### Year 2: Systematization
- [ ] Hire first team member
- [ ] Document all processes
- [ ] Build case studies
- [ ] Target 50%+ recurring revenue

### Year 3+: Scale
- [ ] 15+ stable clients
- [ ] Team of 3-5
- [ ] 70%+ recurring revenue
- [ ] Systems mature enough to delegate
