# Annual Planning Process

## Timeline (Typically Sept-Nov for Jan start)

**September**: Vision & Strategy (Executive team, Board)
- CEO proposes company vision for next year
- Board provides input
- 1-page strategy document created

**October**: Department Planning (VP level)
- Each VP proposes OKRs aligned to company strategy
- Budget requests (headcount, tools, marketing spend)
- Draft finalized mid-October

**November**: Cascade & Finalization
- OKRs finalized with CEO sign-off
- Budgets approved
- Communicated to company (all-hands)
- Hiring and project planning based on plan

## Strategy Document (1 page)

```
2024 STRATEGY - [COMPANY]

Vision: Where we're going (3-5 year)
"Become the leading platform for customer success, serving 10,000+ companies globally"

Mission: Why we exist (unchanged usually)
"Empower companies to drive customer growth through proactive engagement"

Strategic Themes (2-4 for this year):
1. PRODUCT-LED GROWTH
   - What: Shift from sales-led to freemium model
   - Why: Expand TAM, reduce CAC
   - Metrics: 20% of new users from self-serve

2. ENTERPRISE CAPABILITY
   - What: Build security, compliance, SSO
   - Why: Enterprise segment has 5x LTV
   - Metrics: 20% of ARR from enterprise

3. INTERNATIONAL
   - What: Launch in EMEA, hire local teams
   - Why: 60% of TAM outside US
   - Metrics: $500K ARR from international

Key Bets (1-2 big initiatives):
- AI-powered insights (huge opportunity if we nail it)
- Mobile app (customers asking for it)

Key Risks:
- Competitor releases enterprise version
- Macro slowdown affects budgets
- Churn increases if not careful

This clarity drives all planning below.
```

## Company OKRs (Q1 2024)

```
OBJECTIVE 1: Accelerate product-led growth
Key Results:
- 10,000 free users sign up (from current 2,000)
- 15% free-to-paid conversion rate
- 30% of new customers from self-serve (vs. 10% today)

OBJECTIVE 2: Build enterprise capability
Key Results:
- Ship SSO, advanced RBAC, audit logs
- Close 5 enterprise deals (>$100K ACV)
- Achieve SOC2 Type II certification

OBJECTIVE 3: Expand team & culture
Key Results:
- Hire 15 new employees (5 eng, 4 sales, 3 support, 2 marketing, 1 finance)
- Maintain 90%+ employee retention
- Achieve 75+ engagement score (eNPS)
```

## Department OKR Cascade

### Sales Department (VP Sales)

From company OKR "Close 5 enterprise deals", sales translates to:
```
OBJECTIVE: Build enterprise sales motion
Key Results:
- Build pipeline: $2M in enterprise opportunities
- Win rate: 60% (close 3-4 of top 5 deals)
- Close 8 enterprise deals total (including easy ones)
- Expand existing customers: $200K expansion revenue

Success looks like: Enterprise becomes 25% of new ARR (vs. 5% today)
```

### Engineering Department (VP Engineering)

From company OKR "Ship SSO, RBAC, audit logs":
```
OBJECTIVE: Build enterprise security foundation
Key Results:
- Ship SSO/SAML (Q1)
- Ship advanced RBAC (Q2)
- Ship audit logs + compliance dashboard (Q2)
- Achieve SOC2 Type II certification (Q3)
- Zero security vulnerabilities in customer demos

Success looks like: Enterprise customers approve our security posture
```

## Annual Budget Process

### Budget Template

```
MARKETING BUDGET 2024 ($500K total)

Salaries: $250K (2 FTE)
- Head of Marketing: $120K salary
- Content Manager: $90K salary
- Payroll taxes/benefits (20%): $40K

Demand Generation: $150K
- Paid ads (Google, LinkedIn): $60K
- Content creation (freelance): $40K
- Events/sponsorships: $30K
- Tools (HubSpot, Unbounce): $20K

Brand & Communications: $50K
- Brand refresh: $25K
- PR agency: $15K
- Photography/video: $10K

Tools & Infrastructure: $30K
- Analytics, CMS, testing tools: $30K

Contingency (10%): $20K

TOTAL: $500K

ROI targets: Generate $3M in pipeline (6x spend)
```

### Budget Approval Process

1. Each department submits budget (bottom-up)
2. CFO reviews total vs. revenue/runway
3. CEO + CFO trim to acceptable level
4. Board approves (if >$1M or >20% of budget)
5. Locked in Jan 1, reviewed monthly

## Communication & Cascade

### All-Hands Presentation (Jan, 60 min)

```
CEO PRESENTS ANNUAL PLAN

Slide 1: Vision (Where we're going)
Slide 2: 2024 Strategic Themes (focus areas)
Slide 3: Company OKRs (quarterly targets)
Slide 4-8: Department plans (each VP presents 1 slide on their OKRs)
Slide 9: Hiring plan (roles opening, when)
Slide 10: Risks & opportunities (what keeps CEO up at night)
Slide 11: How to get involved (how to align your work)
Slide 12: Q&A
```

### Department Planning (VPs communicate to teams)

Each VP holds department meeting:
- Here's the company OKRs
- Here's what that means for our department
- Here are my OKRs and how they support company OKRs
- Here's how you contribute (link individual work to OKRs)

### Monthly Reviews

First Friday of each month: OKR progress review
- Color-code: Green (on track), Yellow (at risk), Red (off-track)
- Present progress and blockers
- Adjust quarterly targets if market changes

---

**Golden Rule**: Annual planning is not about perfect forecasts (they'll be wrong). It's about alignment. If everyone knows the strategy and their role in it, you move fast. If everyone's guessing, you move slowly and inconsistently.
